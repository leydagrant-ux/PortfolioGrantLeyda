About

An engineer who can also talk to the customer

I graduated from the University of Arkansas in May 2026 with a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering and an aerospace concentration. What I'm best at is structural work: taking a load case, sizing the member that has to carry it, checking it by hand and in FEA, and then building it.

On the capstone I led two of the critical component analyses: the central pin that carries the top arm, and the clevis mount that ties the actuator into it. I then spent that spring sourcing material, machining at both UARK and the sponsor's facility, and assembling the finished machine in their warehouse.

I also spent two years in commission solar sales at Sunder Energy and LGCY Power. That work taught me to explain a technical product to an audience that did not ask for a lesson, to address objections honestly, and to maintain the relationship after the sale. It is a large part of why I am comfortable presenting to a sponsor, and why I am equally interested in design roles and in sales engineering.
