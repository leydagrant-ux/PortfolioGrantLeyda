03 About

Engineer who can talk to the customer

I graduated from the University of Arkansas in May 2026 with a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering and an aerospace concentration. My strongest work is structural: taking a load case, sizing the thing that carries it, proving it with both hand calculations and FEA, and then actually building it.

On the capstone I owned two of the critical component analyses — the central pin that carries the top arm, and the clevis mount that ties the actuator into it. I then spent the spring sourcing material, machining at both UARK and the sponsor's facility, and assembling the finished machine in their warehouse.

Before engineering paid the bills, sales did. Two years of commission solar sales at Sunder Energy and LGCY Power taught me to explain a technical product to somebody who did not ask to be taught, handle the objection honestly, and keep the relationship after the sale. That is not a detour from engineering — it is why I am comfortable in front of a sponsor, and why I am equally interested in design roles and in sales-engineering ones.
